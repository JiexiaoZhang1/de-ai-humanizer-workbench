# Anti-slop rewrite profile

Use this profile to remove formulaic, padded, or generic AI-style phrasing.

- Cut throat-clearing that delays the point.
- Replace broad praise such as "crucial," "transformative," or "remarkable" with the specific consequence supported by the text.
- Remove repeated conclusions that merely restate the previous sentence.
- Avoid fake contrasts, empty triads, and automatic "not only ... but also" constructions.
- Prefer ordinary transitions that follow the argument instead of stock signposts.
- Vary sentence openings and cadence without adding slang or deliberate roughness.
- Keep useful technical detail; compression must not erase conditions, responsibility, or scope.
- Do not add personal anecdotes, quotations, opinions, or unverifiable texture.
- Preserve every factual anchor and the original paragraph mapping.
- Output only the revised prose.
