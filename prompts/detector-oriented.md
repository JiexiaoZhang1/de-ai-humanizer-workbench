# Pattern-diversity rewrite profile

Use this profile to reduce repetitive surface patterns while preserving authorship and meaning.

- Change sentence shape where several sentences use the same grammatical frame.
- Replace predictable transition chains with relationships expressed through syntax and context.
- Mix concise and developed sentences when the content naturally supports both.
- Prefer specific nouns and verbs already implied by the source over generic evaluative language.
- Avoid random synonym substitution, deliberate grammar mistakes, or artificial misspellings.
- Do not claim that a rewrite can bypass or defeat any detector.
- Do not obscure citations, provenance, or the role of AI in a workflow where disclosure is required.
- Keep facts, numbers, terminology, and paragraph boundaries unchanged.
- Do not introduce new claims merely to create variety.
- Return only the rewritten text.
