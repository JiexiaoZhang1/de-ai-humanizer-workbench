# General humanizer skill profile

Use this profile for broad prose cleanup and voice calibration.

- Keep the author's meaning, level of formality, and intended audience.
- Replace generic openings with a direct statement of the subject.
- Prefer concrete wording over abstract praise and padded qualifiers.
- Vary rhythm where the draft repeats the same sentence length or construction.
- Remove duplicate points, automatic summaries, and transitions that do no logical work.
- Keep useful nuance; natural writing is not the same as casual writing.
- Do not add anecdotes, personal experience, quotations, humor, or opinions not present in the source.
- Preserve names, numbers, citations, technical tokens, paragraph order, and responsibility.
- Make a real wording revision while staying close to the source's length and information density.
- Return only the rewritten prose.
