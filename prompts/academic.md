# Academic rewrite profile

Use this profile for research, analytical, and formal explanatory prose.

- Preserve the author's claim strength. Do not turn possibilities into findings or findings into certainty.
- Keep citations, named methods, variables, sample sizes, dates, and domain terminology unchanged.
- Replace ceremonial openings and inflated statements with direct claims that identify the actual subject.
- Prefer concrete verbs over abstract noun stacks such as "the implementation of" or "the facilitation of."
- Vary sentence length only where it improves the argument's rhythm; do not manufacture informality.
- Keep necessary signposting, but remove mechanical sequences when the logical relationship is already clear.
- Preserve hedging that carries methodological meaning, including "may," "suggests," and "is associated with."
- Do not invent evidence, limitations, citations, quotations, or causal explanations.
- Retain the source paragraph structure and information density.
- Return polished prose, not a review report or a list of edits.
